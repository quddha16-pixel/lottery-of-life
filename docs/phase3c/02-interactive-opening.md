# Phase 3C milestone 2: an interactive opening

This milestone builds on the survival/feedback PR. It is stacked for review; nothing is merged or released.

## Visible changes

The active screen starts with the animal, two useful choices and current circumstances. Dependent animals get a direct care/learning choice plus an explicit next-developmental-moment control. Independent animals get two prioritized actions; further choices remain in a labeled disclosure. A 390 × 664 regression checks both useful choices above fixed navigation, not just one.

Scene targets open keyboard-accessible native dialogs for the animal, real family interactions and biological resources. Available actions dispatch the same validated engine commands. Map destinations open an immediate route dialog with cost, escort/access conditions and a disabled explanation when travel is impossible. This replaces the off-screen route detail panel.

Structured results are visible beside the choices and can be expanded to show elapsed phases and actual changes. Critical needs take priority. Field notes are optional, reachable from active play, record read topics without spending time/random draws or awarding currency, and retain sourced facts.

A shared opening profile covers all thirteen current species, including bumblebee. The profile explains the actual life stage, first loop, biological stocks and upcoming developmental opportunities. It does not grant egg/larval mobility or early adult abilities. Detailed narration keeps ecological context; both modes use identical commands/rules.

## Simulation and compatibility

Watching an important moment simulates every intervening phase, including mortality, nursery care, ecology and nearby animals. It stops for a newly critical need, significant encounter, major injury, development or death. The audit ant and bumblebee seeds develop through their real brood stages in a few explicit transitions rather than dozens of care clicks. These sampled openings do not establish survival rates for all seeds.

New founders gain persistent visited-place knowledge. Species use several different bounded connected graphs, with actual route availability/cost consequences. Old saved maps remain visible; existing nodes/links are not rebuilt. Travel and autonomous movement share knowledge accounting. Newly authorized sensory learning is now actually exposed in the UI, rather than filtered out.

No save version bump or storage-key change is required. New knowledge/read flags use bounded existing biological skills. No progression reward is granted for reading. Existing v1/v2/v3, packs, family, calendar and currency tests remain.

## Evidence and boundaries

Unit coverage includes all thirteen profiles, real ant/bumblebee development, mortality during observation, learning effects, urgent action priority, graph connectivity, legacy map visibility and read-only presentation. New browser cases use the normal birth UI: fresh wolf controls/keyboard/locked routes; fresh ant development, gardening, travel and harvesting; and optional discovery persistence without elapsed time.

Production-bundle browser tests and local screenshots are additional developer evidence, not Quentin's iPhone review. Playable and Released remain pending. Catalog-wide original animal illustrations, stronger location art and deeper first-session demonstrations follow in milestone 3; the ten existing specimen fallbacks have not been called finished artwork here.
