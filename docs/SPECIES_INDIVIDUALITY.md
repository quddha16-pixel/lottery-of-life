# All 13 species — mechanics and reference assumptions

References checked 17 November 2026. Facts are paraphrased; no reference artwork was copied. Species accounts, conservation organizations and wildlife-agency summaries inform qualitative rules. No source is claimed to validate the game's energy points, phase lengths, success probabilities, developmental cutoffs or reward thresholds. Those are bounded design parameters requiring further calibration.

All species use one phase resolver. Each has saved, independent biological stores and skills, stage milestones, a distinct location profile, a bounded mastery achievement, and exposure to the same permanent mortality system. Personality, inherited traits and experience continue to influence feeding, risk and autonomous decisions.

## Interpretation and limits

- Locations are five connected habitat archetypes within the historical generator's compatible region. They are not a full home range, migration route or actual historical site survey. A glacial wolf scene uses open scrub/grassland rather than assuming a modern forest.
- Ambient conditions, local resource depletion, illness risk, injury, age and environmental pressure remain connected to survival. Each species has additional stateful risks or constraints; none gains immunity. Food at a truly empty resource cannot be obtained through a perception bonus. Drinking does not create water in a dry patch.
- Breeding tracks a sampled future cohort for either sex. The saved legacy field is named `pregnantUntil`, but male play represents the partner's gestation/incubation. Clutch/litter counts are intentionally small samples. Detailed pairing, paternity, spatial maternal fidelity and endocrine regulation are not simulated.
- NPCs are local lightweight agents with coarse condition/reproduction, plus aggregate background populations. Their parameters are not yet species-complete physiological models. Player biology modules provide the catalog's primary differentiated mechanics.
- Developmental and travel thresholds are conservative play boundaries. They are not universal ages of emergence, weaning, fledging or dispersal. Existing saves keep their exact ages; corrected thresholds change current eligibility, not saved dates or elapsed life.
- The mastery bonus is bounded to one species achievement per lifetime. Newborn abandonment still earns zero Coins, and reward settlement remains idempotent.

Automated evidence is in `src/game/biology.test.ts`, `src/game/wildlife.test.ts` and production-bundle browser flows for all 13 species. Tests assert resource transfer, capabilities, other individuals' costs, persistent location data, mortality and progression; they do not merely compare labels.

## Phase 3C reference/parameter corrections

Elephant dependence is not a ban on every physical activity. The older early return hid even one-year digging until the juvenile stage at three years. Shared eligibility now lets a calf join wallowing from day 30 and help uncover shallow water from day 365, only beside a capable relative at a suitable wet location. The existing 365-day digging threshold is retained; 30 days is a conservative gameplay boundary, not a universal measured onset. Digging consumes the finite groundwater reserve and the relative's energy as well as the calf's. It does not grant independent nursing/foraging abilities or guarantee water.

References: [San Diego Zoo elephant overview](https://animals.sandiegozoo.org/animals/elephant), [SDZ Wildlife Alliance development fact sheet](https://ielc.libguides.com/sdzg/factsheets/african_elephant/reproduction), and [Freeman et al. 2010 captive calf survey](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20391463/). Wild summaries and captive observations report different trunk-use timings; these are context-dependent observations, not one precise universal drinking age. This change does not invent a new independent-drinking deadline.

Leafcutter workers now carry a bounded leaf load before depositing at a nursery/garden. Leaves are not instant food; the existing fungus conversion still applies. Bumblebee nests use rounded wax cells/pots in the illustration, not honeybee hexagonal comb. Nestling robin food from a parent now fills the same bounded night reserve as its later independent feeding. This fixes the accidental exclusion of parental meals; it does not introduce free food or adult capabilities.
